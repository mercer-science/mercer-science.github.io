Does the adhesin layer on the surface of *B. henselae* physically block the secretion
system underneath it? A published hypothesis says yes, from phenotypes alone, and no
tomogram of any species in this genus exists. I scoped and proposed the project and
Dr. Jensen approved it. Nothing has been cultured and no grids have been frozen; the work
starts once the biosafety committee approves the protocol. Full write-up on the Physical
Sciences tab.
