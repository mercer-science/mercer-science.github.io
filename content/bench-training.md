**Bacterial culture and plasmid work.** In the Jensen Lab's September 2026 bootcamp I grew
*E. coli* BL21 Star (DE3) carrying a human ferritin H expression plasmid, made LB and 2×YT
media, and ran overnight starters, induction, and a plasmid miniprep on the lab's protocol.
I have done the culture steps. Purification and grid preparation come next.

**Cryo-EM sample preparation and imaging.** Thermo Fisher's Resolution Evolution course in
October 2026, with time on the Vitrobot for plunge-freezing and on the Krios.

**Electron microscopy.** Two years on a Thermo Fisher G4 Verios, plus a Helios and
immersion mode, imaging thin films and measuring their thickness in ImageJ. I taught two
newer students to run both.

**Vacuum and surface analysis.** Leak checking, bakeouts, and flange work on a chamber that
runs near 10⁻¹⁰ Torr, with X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy and mass spectrometry attached
to it.

**Courses with real lab components.** Advanced Biochemistry Methods 1 (CHEM 584, a
master's-level course, in progress), Biochemistry Methods (CHEM 384), Molecular and
Cellular Biology Lab (MMBIO 241), and Advanced Physiology Lab (CELL 363). The lecture
courses behind them are Genetics (PWS 340), Molecular Biology (MMBIO 240), and Mechanisms
of Molecular Biology (CHEM 482). Structural Biochemistry (CHEM 489) and Advanced
Biochemistry Methods 2 (CHEM 586) come in Winter 2027.
