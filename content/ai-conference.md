I attended the AI for Social Innovation Conference at Aspen Grove on October 21, 2026, run
by BYU's Ballard Center, as the BYU AI Association's representative. The organizers wanted
someone from the association and I was given a seat at the Data Sovereignty and Inclusion
working table, alongside a UN ambassador, the chief operating officer of a Māori media
organization that built a community-owned speech model, and people from industry and other
universities. The table's question was who owns community data, the models trained on it,
and the value those models create.
