Biochemistry major with a computer science minor at Brigham Young University, graduating
April 2028. I design for a narrow and demanding set of users: physicians and medical
physicists who have minutes, not hours, and who need to know whether a number can be
trusted. The diffusion MRI workspace I built at UT Southwestern is the main case study,
from the problem through the iterations to what a physician could do afterward that they
could not do before.

I also build what I design, which keeps the designs honest about what is possible.
