Biochemistry major with a computer science minor (machine learning emphasis) at Brigham
Young University, graduating April 2028. I rebuilt UT Southwestern's diffusion MRI
processing pipeline into a web application that ran 76 patients in about 2.5 days and has
since moved to the team that maintains the hospital's clinical software. The extraction and
figure pipelines behind four radiation oncology manuscripts are mine too, in C#, Python,
and R.

I write software because the science stalls without it, not the other way around. That
shows up in what I build: tools somebody else can run after I leave.
