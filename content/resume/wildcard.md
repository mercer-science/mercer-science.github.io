Biochemistry major with a computer science minor at Brigham Young University, graduating
April 2028. Two years of surface chemistry at the bench, a published paper, two
cryo-electron tomography projects I scoped in Dr. Jensen's lab, four radiation oncology
studies at UT Southwestern, a clinical imaging application now headed into hospital use,
nine months as a medical assistant, and a seat at a UN-adjacent working table on data
sovereignty.

The range is not a collection of interests. It is one habit applied in different rooms:
find where the work is stuck, build the thing that unsticks it, and write it down so it
outlasts me.
