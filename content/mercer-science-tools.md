I release research tooling under the Mercer Science organization on GitHub as it becomes
good enough for someone else to use.

**paper-engine** is the main one: skills, scripts, and strategies that take an academic
paper from an idea toward a draft. It covers the parts that are mechanical but easy to get
wrong, such as keeping an outline, figures, statistics, and references consistent with each
other while the draft changes underneath them. I built it out of the manuscript work at UT
Southwestern, where I rewrote one paper more than eight times.

**github-ai-project-manager** is a set of skills and hooks that let an AI command-line
agent maintain a research project on GitHub on its own, so the record of what happened
keeps up with the work.

**AI-In-Research** holds the proposal behind the BYU AI Association group I lead.

More will go up as it stops being specific to my own machine. The repositories are at
[github.com/mercer-science](https://github.com/mercer-science).
