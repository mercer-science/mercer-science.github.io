The lab courses behind the research, as opposed to the research itself. Each one is a
semester of bench work with its own techniques.

#### CHEM 584, Advanced Biochemistry Methods 1

A master's-level methods course, in progress this semester. The class project runs a full
cloning workflow, from primer design and PCR through transformation, miniprep, and
sequencing, and then moves to human cell culture and induced protein expression.

#### CHEM 384, Biochemistry Methods

Protein purification and characterization: expression, chromatography, electrophoresis,
and the assays that tell you whether any of it worked.

#### MMBIO 241, Molecular and Cellular Biology Lab

Bacterial culture, transformation, plasmid preparation, and the molecular techniques the
Jensen Lab bootcamp later assumed I already knew.

#### CELL 363, Advanced Physiology Lab

Measurement on living systems, and the part of lab work that is about instrument error and
experimental design rather than pipetting.

#### Winter 2027

CHEM 489 Structural Biochemistry, and CHEM 586 Advanced Biochemistry Methods 2, where the
TAs prepare cell lines carrying the mutants our class cloned this year.

#### Lecture Courses Behind Them

Genetics (PWS 340), Molecular Biology (MMBIO 240), Mechanisms of Molecular Biology
(CHEM 482), Biochemistry Fundamentals (CHEM 381M), and Biophysical Chemistry (CHEM 468).
