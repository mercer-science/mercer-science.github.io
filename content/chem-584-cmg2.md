In CHEM 584, Advanced Biochemistry Methods 1 (a master's-level course), my group cloned a
deletion of the cytoplasmic actin-binding domain of CMG2, a human cell-surface receptor and
one of the anthrax toxin receptors. We do every step ourselves, from primer design and PCR
through transformation, miniprep, and sequencing, and whole-plasmid sequencing confirmed
our clone. This semester we are practicing human cell culture and induced protein
expression with the wild-type gene; next semester we move to cell lines carrying the
mutants.

The deletion was the class's assigned mutant. We designed our own primers, and they were
the only CMG2 primers in the class that worked.
