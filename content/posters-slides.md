I have presented six times across three fields, which means designing for three different
audiences who read a poster differently.

The Utah CryoEM Symposium poster, *Can an AI Agent Collapse the Cryo-ET Learning Curve?*,
had to make a claim about AI credible to structural biologists who are rightly skeptical of
it. The design answer was to lead with the logged record of what the agent got right and
wrong, rather than with the pipeline diagram.

For the Simmons Center talk in August 2026 I built the slides for the summer's radiation
oncology work, and for the BYU Medical Society poster that month I shared the design with
the other intern. Earlier, the Stowers chemistry work went to the BYU Student Research
Conference twice and to the American Chemical Society spring meeting in Atlanta.
