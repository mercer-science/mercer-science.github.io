**Jensen Lab, BYU, August 2026 to present.** Two cryo-electron tomography projects I scoped
and proposed, and that Dr. Jensen approved: the surface architecture of *Bartonella
henselae*, asking whether its adhesin layer blocks the secretion system underneath, and the
apical complex of *Babesia duncani*, which would be the first tomography in that genus.
Both are **ongoing and in biosafety approval**, so nothing has been cultured or frozen yet.
I also run the lab's AI cryo-ET agent project, which I presented as a poster at the Utah
CryoEM Symposium in October 2026.

**Stowers Lab, BYU, April 2024 to May 2026.** Metal-organic framework thin films grown by
spin coating and characterized by electron microscopy, published in *Langmuir* with me as
second author. I helped build and maintain a flow reactor and an ultra-high-vacuum chamber
running near 10⁻¹⁰ Torr with X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy and mass spectrometry, and
trained two newer students on spin coating and microscopy. A second manuscript on the
vacuum-chamber chemistry is in preparation.

**Bench training.** Vitrobot and Krios through Thermo Fisher's Resolution Evolution course,
bacterial culture and plasmid work in the Jensen Lab's apoferritin bootcamp, electron
microscopy and film analysis in the Stowers Lab, plus the biochemistry lab sequence at BYU
through CHEM 584, a master's-level methods course.
