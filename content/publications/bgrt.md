First author.

Biology-guided radiotherapy follows the tumor's live PET signal during delivery instead of
padding the target with margin for breathing, so the treated volume can be smaller. Across
17 patients we measured how much smaller, and what it costs: the target came out 19 percent
smaller, and the reduction grew with how far the tumor moved. The trade is a wider
intermediate-dose region and higher spinal cord and heart maxima, which originate in
planning rather than in delivery.
