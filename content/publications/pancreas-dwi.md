Seventy-six patients processed through the pipeline, 23 of them with both complete
diffusion imaging and outcome data, paired with chart review for response and failure. It
is where the lung study's threshold tool starts being tested against what actually happened
to patients, on a schedule that treats every two or three days rather than weeks apart.
