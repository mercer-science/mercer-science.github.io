First author, with Chitral Chatterjee and Dr. Grant Jensen. Presented as a poster at the
Utah CryoEM Symposium, October 8, 2026.

Cryo-electron tomography processing is a chain of a dozen specialized programs that takes
months to learn. We put a general-purpose AI coding agent at the controls of that chain on
39 tilt series of *E. coli*, with a human approving or overriding every parameter, and
logged each recommendation as accepted, overridden, or an error a human caught. The point
is to measure where the agent genuinely helps a newcomer and where it needs someone who
already knows the science.
