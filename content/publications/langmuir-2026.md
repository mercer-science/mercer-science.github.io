Second author. Ralph AD, **Mercer RW**, Hall SB, Butikofer BC, Collins CS, Sturgill CJ,
Minson PS, Stefik M, Stowers KJ. *Langmuir* 2026;42(6):4475–4488.
[doi:10.1021/acs.langmuir.5c05182](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.langmuir.5c05182)

Metal-organic framework films usually need a self-assembled monolayer to nucleate on. We
grew copper and copper-zinc films directly on a bare substrate by spin coating at ambient
pressure, then showed how synthesis temperature controls coverage, crystallite size, and
how much of the second metal ends up in the film. I did film synthesis, electron
microscopy, and film-thickness analysis.
