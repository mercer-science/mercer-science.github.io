Co-author.

Forty patients treated with stereotactic radiation for early-stage lung cancer on an
adaptive platform, with three plan states compared on the same daily anatomy at every
fraction: the approved plan, the scheduled plan, and the adapted plan actually delivered.
The question is which patients gain enough from adaptation to justify a workflow that
roughly doubles time on the table, and whether anything measurable at simulation predicts
it.
