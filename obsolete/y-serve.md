I direct Early Learning Essentials, a BYU Y-Serve program that puts student volunteers into
preschool classrooms serving families with low incomes. I started as a program director in
August 2024 and have been lead program director since March 2025, which means recruiting
volunteers, running the leadership team, scheduling the classroom shifts, and keeping the
partnership with the Provo center working. I still take classroom shifts myself.
