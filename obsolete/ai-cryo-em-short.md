In Dr. Jensen's lab I am testing whether a general-purpose AI coding agent can run the
cryo-electron tomography processing pipeline while a human keeps every scientific decision.
The agent proposes, installs, and runs each tool; I approve or override each parameter; and
every recommendation is logged as accepted, overridden, or an error I caught. I presented
it as a poster at the Utah CryoEM Symposium in October 2026, and the full write-up sits on
the Physical Sciences tab.
