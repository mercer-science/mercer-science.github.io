Biochemistry researcher who builds software for the lab and the clinic.

I am a biochemistry major at Brigham Young University, minoring in computer science with a machine learning emphasis. I work in a research lab run by Dr. Grant Jensen, I rebuilt a diffusion MRI processing system that is now moving into clinical use at UT Southwestern, and I help lead the AI in Research group of the BYU AI Association. Background

I am on BYU's biochemistry track and have worked through the major's chemistry and biochemistry sequence to CHEM 584. That coursework runs from organic and physical chemistry into advanced biochemistry, so I am comfortable with the wet-lab fundamentals as well as the quantitative side. I expect to graduate in April 2028 with a 3.99 GPA.

Right now I work in a graduate-level research lab, alongside master's and doctoral students on projects that are their full-time thesis work. I have been trusted with the same instruments and the same problems they handle, which is unusual for an undergraduate and is the kind of footing I would bring to a demanding team.

What I Work On

The lab

In Dr. Jensen's lab I work on cryo-electron tomography, imaging bacteria down to the level of individual molecules inside the cell. Two threads: using an AI coding agent to run the heavy image-processing pipeline, and imaging bacteria associated with tick-borne disease. I trained on the Vitrobot and the Krios microscope and presented a poster on the AI work at the Utah CryoEM Symposium.

The clinic

As a clinical research assistant at UT Southwestern I led retrospective studies of new radiation therapy technology and rebuilt the department's diffusion MRI pipeline into a web application. It processed every patient in a study in about two and a half days of server time, and the hospital's IT group moved it to the team that runs clinical software.

Tools and teams

I build research tooling under the Mercer Science organization on GitHub, I lead the weekly AI in Research meeting for the BYU AI Association, and I direct an early-childhood literacy program through BYU Y-Serve. I also sat on a data sovereignty working table at BYU's AI for Social Innovation Conference.
